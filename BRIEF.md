# AI Product Design Challenge — Bosshire Design Test

## Brief Overview
Design a **mobile AI-powered job portal experience** that helps job seekers make better decisions during their job search.
Choose **one meaningful problem** within the job-seeking journey and solve it deeply.

> **Key Rule:** The goal is not simply to "add AI" to an existing job portal. The goal is to identify where AI creates a **meaningfully better experience** that wouldn't be possible through static UI alone. **Do not simply build a chatbot.**

## Potential Areas of Exploration (Choose One or Define Your Own)
1. **Finding the right job:** Filtering through noise to identify roles aligned with values, growth, or compensation.
2. **Evaluating job fit:** Understanding real skill match, culture fit, or hidden role expectations before applying.
3. **Improving a CV or application:** Tailoring applications to specific jobs with dynamic alignment.
4. **Prioritizing job opportunities:** Deciding where to invest time and effort across multiple open roles.
5. **Managing applications:** Tracking progress, follow-ups, and managing response pipelines.
6. **Understanding application outcomes:** Deriving actionable insight from rejections or ghosting.

## Core Requirements
### 1. Problem & Product Thinking (30%)
- **Target User:** Who are you designing for?
- **Problem Statement:** What specific friction point are they experiencing?
- **Importance & Opportunity:** Why is this problem worth solving now?
- **Proposed Solution:** How does your concept solve this problem?
- **Key Assumptions & Prioritization:** What assumptions are you making? What did you prioritize and why?
- **Scope Trade-offs:** What did you intentionally leave out to keep the solution focused?

### 2. AI & Human Collaboration (25%)
- **Roles:** What does the AI do versus what decisions remain firmly with the user?
- **Inputs & Outputs:** What data does the AI draw from, and what does it recommend, analyze, or generate?
- **User Control:** How can the user inspect, edit, reject, or override AI outputs?
- **Handling Uncertainty:** How does the UI accommodate low confidence, missing data, or inaccurate AI outputs?

### 3. UX & Interaction Design (25%)
- Core journey: **Problem → AI Interaction → User Decision → Action → Outcome**
- Critical states & edge cases:
  - Empty states
  - Loading & AI processing states
  - Low-confidence or no-recommendation results
  - Error handling and user corrections
  - Success state

### 4. UI Design & Craft (20%)
- **Format:** Mobile experience (iOS or Android, `393×852` / `390×844`).
- **Scope:** 5–7 key screens demonstrating the end-to-end core flow.
- **Focus Areas:** Information hierarchy, intuitive interaction, visual polish, component consistency, accessibility, and thoughtful UI micro-details.

### 5. Deliverables
1. **Product Concept Summary**
2. **User Flow Diagram**
3. **Mobile UI Screens (5–7 polished high-fidelity mobile screens)**
4. **AI Interaction Breakdown**
5. **Design Rationale (2–3 critical design decisions)**
